🍼 Evaluating at Birth

This is how I recommend we evaluate alpacas at birth.

At birth, do not overemphasize fiber fineness.

The first assessment is structural.

Most Important Birth Question

Does he/she look like a structurally correct male/female with the raw materials to become a dense, well-organized Suri?

Don’t make the mistake of selecting him—or eliminating him—based primarily on how fine he feels at a few days old.
